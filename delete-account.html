UMPIRE VISION

Delete your account

You can delete your Umpire Vision account and all its data at any time.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Umpire Vision.
  2. On Home, open your Profile.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted straight away, and the app starts fresh.

On the web (if you no longer have the app)

Email ayushs125@gmail.com (Android) or thunderlinks72@gmail.com (iPhone) with the subject Delete my account, and include one of:

  • the email address you signed in with (Google or Apple), or
  • your display name on the leaderboard.

We'll confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Everything linked to your account: your account ID and sign-in, display name, scores, calls, reaction times, levels, streaks, stats and badges. Leaderboards no longer show you.

What we keep

Nothing linked to you. Basic server logs held by our hosting provider for security expire on their own after a short period.
