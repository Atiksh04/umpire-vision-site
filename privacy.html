UMPIRE VISION

Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026

Umpire Vision is a cricket umpiring game. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. In short: we keep what the game needs to save your progress and run the leaderboards, nothing is sold, there are no ads, and you can delete everything at any time.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
An anonymous account IDCreated when you first open the appTo save your progress, scores and streaks
Your display nameIf you choose oneShown next to your score on leaderboards
Your email addressOnly if you sign in with Google (or Apple)To sign you in and keep your progress on a new phone
Gameplay: your calls, scores, reaction times, levels, streaks and badgesWhile you playScoring, leaderboards and your stats
Basic server logs (IP address, device type, time)When the app talks to our serverSecurity and keeping the service running; kept briefly by our hosting provider

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or files, advertising ID, or payment details. Share cards are made on your phone and handed to your phone's share sheet; they are never uploaded to us. Streak reminders are scheduled on your phone; we don't send push notifications.

Who can see it

Other players see only your display name and score on leaderboards. If you haven't picked a name, a generated one such as "Umpire 4821" is shown instead. Nothing else about you is visible to other players.

The daily leaderboard also shows practice players while the app is new, so the board isn't empty. They aren't real people and they give way as real players join.

Who processes it

We don't sell or rent your data or use it for advertising, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. These services process data for us, only to run the app:

  • Supabase: our database and accounts, hosted in Singapore.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: only if you choose to sign in.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: to deliver the app.

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and everything linked to it (name, scores, calls, streaks, stats and badges) is deleted straight away. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a short period for security.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Or request it on the web: delete your account.

Children

Umpire Vision is for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by contacting us. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or India's DPDP Act) you may have further rights, which we will honour.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
