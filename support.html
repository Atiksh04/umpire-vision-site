How do I play?

Watch the ball until it hits the pad, then swipe right for OUT or left for NOT OUT. The ball tracker then shows where it pitched, where it hit the pad, and whether it was going on to hit the stumps.

How is LBW decided?

As in a real review, a batter is out only if all three checks go against them: the ball didn't pitch outside leg stump, it hit the pad in line (or outside off with no shot played), and it was going on to hit the stumps.

How does scoring work?

A right call scores points, with more for close calls and quick calls. A run of right calls multiplies your score. Wrong calls end the run.

What is the daily over?

Six balls, the same for every player, new each day at midnight India time. You get one attempt, and today's leaderboard ranks everyone who played.

What are replays?

In levels and Endless you can watch a ball again before you call it, twice a day. A replayed ball doesn't earn the quick-call bonus.

Will I lose my progress on a new phone?

Sign in with Google from Profile to keep your progress, then sign in with the same account on your new phone.

How do I change my name?

Profile → Your name. Names are 3 to 16 letters, numbers or underscores, and can be changed once a week.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account, or see delete your account.
